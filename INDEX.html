    🩺 Health Instructions & Diet Tips

    🩸 Blood Sugar (Fasting)

    Normal: 70–99 mg/dL

    Prediabetes: 100–125 mg/dL

    Diabetes: 126 mg/dL or above

    Diet Tips

    • 🥗 Eat whole grains, vegetables, and fruits.
    • 🚫 Reduce sweets, soft drinks, and sugary foods.
    • 💧 Drink enough water every day.
    • 🚶 Walk or exercise for at least 30 minutes daily.

    ❤️ Blood Pressure

    Normal: Below 120/80 mmHg

    Elevated: 120–129 / Below 80 mmHg

    High: 130/80 mmHg or above

    Diet Tips

    • 🧂 Reduce salt intake.
    • 🥬 Eat leafy vegetables and fruits.
    • 🏃 Exercise regularly.
    • 😴 Get 7–9 hours of sleep.

    💓 Heart Rate

    Normal Resting Rate: 60–100 bpm

    Healthy Tips

    • ❤️ Exercise regularly.
    • 💧 Stay hydrated.
    • 😌 Reduce stress with relaxation techniques.
    • 🚭 Avoid smoking.

    ⚖️ BMI (Body Mass Index)

    • Below 18.5 – Underweight
    • 18.5–24.9 – Healthy Weight
    • 25–29.9 – Overweight
    • 30 and above – Obesity

    🥗 Healthy Lifestyle

    • 🍎 Eat a balanced diet.
    • 💧 Drink 2–3 liters of water daily.
    • 🚶 Exercise for 30 minutes every day.
    • 😴 Sleep 7–9 hours each night.
    • 🚭 Avoid smoking and limit alcohol.
    • 🩺 Get regular health check-ups.

    ⚠️ Important Note

    This application provides health information for educational purposes only. It is not a medical diagnosis. If your readings are consistently abnormal or you feel unwell, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
